# Article rewrite brief

0. Keywords: pick one main keyword for the article and 3–5 related questions people search (use Google Suggest and People Also Ask). Put the main keyword in the title tag, first paragraph, one H2 and the meta description, naturally. Answer the related questions in the article.
1. Answer first: the first paragraph directly answers the title's question.
2. Local: real Adelaide/SA detail (suburbs, climate, CBS, SA rules), never generic.
3. Trust: specific numbers only if confirmed; add a short "Sources" section with official or manufacturer sources only (CBS, PlanSA, SafeWork SA, BlueScope, BoM, YourHome), opening in a new tab. Never link competitor roofers.
4. Scannable: clear headings, short paragraphs, lists or a table where useful.
5. Depth: pass the "no more Googling" test, so a homeowner shouldn't need to search anything else on this topic.
6. Own angle: don't duplicate another article on the site; link to it instead.
7. Links: keep the link to its cluster hub, add 2–4 natural links to related articles and service pages with varied anchors, and make sure at least one other article links in to this one.
8. Keep the existing image, byline, schema, Open Graph, sidebar and contact sections.
9. Write a new meta description under 160 characters.
10. Facts: soften or remove anything you can't confirm, and list what you softened.
11. Image alt text: every image gets a short, accurate description, naturally including the main keyword where it fits.
12. FAQ: end the article with 3–5 short FAQs answering the related 'People Also Ask' questions, with FAQPage schema.
13. Original asset: every article includes at least one original asset (a simple SVG diagram, a printable checklist, a comparison table, or the embedded calculator where cost is discussed).
14. Target score: 46+/50. If below, list what's holding it back, fix what you can, and re-score.

## Scoring

Score the article out of 10 on each of criteria 1–5 (out of 50), both BEFORE and AFTER the rewrite, and say whether it passes the "no more Googling" test.

## Report

Show the scores, the full new text, all links in and out, and the facts checked. Build and verify, but don't commit until approved.
